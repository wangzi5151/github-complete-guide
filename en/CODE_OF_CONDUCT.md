# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In order to create an open and friendly environment, we pledge to:

- Respect everyone
- Accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using inclusive and friendly language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information
- Other unprofessional or unethical conduct

## Enforcement

Abuse can be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and a response will be given that is necessary and appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the following contexts:

- The project repository
- The project's Issues and Pull Requests
- The project's Discussions
- The project's social media accounts
- Public spaces at project events
